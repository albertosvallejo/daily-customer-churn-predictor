Executive takeaway

What this means: churn prioritization is driven mainly by purchase cadence, recency, and customer economic signals. The model is strongest when used to rank who should be reviewed first, not as a literal causal explanation of why a customer will churn.

How to use it: combine the score with the dominant driver group to choose whether the next intervention should emphasize reactivation urgency, repeat-purchase nurturing, or value-bundle recovery.

Interpretation rule

Explainability is descriptive of the model behavior. It supports portfolio decision-making and action design, but it is not a proof of customer-level causality.
