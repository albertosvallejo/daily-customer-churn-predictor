Assumptions used

  • Response assumptions by risk tier: Conservative (HIGH 4%, MEDIUM 2.5%, LOW 0.8%), Base case (HIGH 7%, MEDIUM 4.5%, LOW 1.5%), Upside (HIGH 10%, MEDIUM 6%, LOW 2%).

  • Discount cost proxy: 35% of implied discount value over the saved-margin proxy.
  • Channel / touch cost proxy: lightweight per-send handling plus surcharges for free shipping and VIP human-touch cases.

Interpretation

  • HIGH should remain the first experimental tier because it concentrates the richest intervention logic and the best expected recovery opportunity.
  • MEDIUM is better framed as a lower-cost nurturing layer than as a discount-heavy segment.
  • LOW is large in volume, but the scenario economics suggest loyalty / monitoring use is safer than aggressive retention spend.
  • Base-case caution: the current base-case scenario is still negative, which is analytically useful because it argues for controlled internal validation before scaling real-send automation.
Publication-ready message: the project can credibly show a scenario-based ROI layer for stakeholder discussion, while still presenting causal uplift estimation and ROI-based threshold optimization as explicit next upgrades. This supports a strong “internal-pilot-first, production-layer-later” roadmap.
